Keep playing,keep learning

The Seven Sisters International Music Workshop in October 2026 was a beginning. To keep that progress going, we are planning private and small-group lessons, taught online by UK-based international faculty, for workshop participants and other aspiring musicians across North East India.

What we are planning

Two ways to learn

Lessons start online with UK-based faculty, scheduled for evenings and weekends in India. We will match learners to teachers and groups once we know who is interested, on which instruments and at what level.

Private lessons

One-to-one lessons shaped around the learner: technique, repertoire, musicianship and, for those who want it, preparation for graded exams.

Small-group lessons

Small groups of learners at a similar level, combining playing together with theory, aural and sight-reading. A more affordable way to learn with international faculty.

Instruments

Initially violin, viola, cello, piano and voice. We will add further instruments as interest grows, so tell us what you play even if it isn't listed.

When

Online, at times that suit India: weekday evenings and weekends. Early evening in India is early afternoon in the UK.

This is a register of interest, not a booking. Fees, timetables and teacher allocation will be shared with everyone who registers. Places will depend on faculty availability.
A pathway, not just lessons

Learning that builds year on year

Lessons follow the Seven Sisters Conservatoire curriculum. Learners who want a recognised qualification can prepare for Trinity College London graded exams. Each year, at least one of the pieces a learner prepares comes from the music of Assam or the wider North East.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to have taken part in the October 2026 workshop?

No. Workshop participants are warmly encouraged to continue, but lessons are open to any aspiring musician in North East India.

How much will lessons cost?

Fees are being finalised and will depend on the format. Small-group lessons will cost less per learner than private lessons. Everyone who registers will receive the details before being asked to commit.

What do I need for an online lesson?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a camera, a stable internet connection and a quiet room. A stand to hold the device at the right angle helps the teacher see posture and technique.

Can I take a Trinity College London exam?

Yes, if you would like to. Teachers can prepare you for Trinity graded exams. Exams are booked and examined by Trinity College London, and exam fees are paid separately.
