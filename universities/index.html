Seven Sisters Conservatoire is building a single path from first lessons to postgraduate study, benchmarked to Trinity College London qualifications, with the music of Assam and the North East studied, notated and performed at every stage. Where this started

Built on the October 2026 workshop

In October 2026, the Seven Sisters International Music Workshop brought international faculty to Srimanta Sankaradeva Kalakshetra in Guwahati to mentor young musicians from across the North East and perform with them. The final performance was covered by DD Assam, Prag News and News 365. It confirmed that the region has exceptional musical talent, and too few pathways for it.

The curriculum

From first lessons to postgraduate study

Seven stages, each with an external benchmark. Graded exams and diplomas are set and examined by Trinity College London, with Seven Sisters Conservatoire (SSC) teaching and preparing learners for them. Indian and North East instruments, which Trinity does not examine, are certified by SSC on the same grade framework. Degrees will be awarded by a university partner.

Three pieces, one from home

Every grade follows Trinity's structure of pieces, technical work and supporting tests. SSC adds one rule: of the three pieces prepared each year, at least one comes from Assam or the North East.

Ensemble and stage

Choir, string orchestra, chamber groups or band every term, and at least one public performance a year.

Ethnomusicology

A strand running through every stage that studies, notates and codifies the classical, folk and popular music of India and the North East in a Western context.

Two tracks

Classical & Jazz and Contemporary (Rock & Pop), following Trinity's syllabuses, with the ethnomusicology strand common to both.

University membership
