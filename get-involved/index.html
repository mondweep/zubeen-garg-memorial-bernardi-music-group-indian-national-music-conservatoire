What comesnext

After the first Seven Sisters International Music Workshop in October 2026, we are planning two events for 2027: a second edition in Guwahati, and a showcase in London of the art and culture of Assam and North East India. We would like your ideas, and your help to make them happen.

2027

Two events, two cities

Second edition · planned
Monday 31 May – Saturday 5 June 2027
Guwahati, Assam

The second Seven Sisters International Music Workshop: a week of lessons, masterclasses and rehearsals with international faculty for young musicians from across the North East, ending in a public final performance.

We are especially interested in ideas that reach learners outside Guwahati.

Proposed
September 2027
London, United Kingdom

A showcase of the music, art and culture of Assam and North East India for audiences in the UK, bringing together artists from the region, the diaspora and the musicians of the October 2026 workshop.

We welcome ideas on venues, programme, artists and partners.

Both events are subject to suitable sponsorship being secured beforehand. Dates, venues and programmes will be confirmed once funding is in place.
For broadcasters and media houses

A music classroom for every district

We want to take the workshop beyond the hall. Our idea is a live teaching event with an audience: a presenter leads the session in Guwahati, young musicians play on stage, and international faculty join by video to teach and give feedback. Broadcast and streamed across Assam and the North East, it would bring the same learning to students in remote districts who cannot travel.

We are looking for broadcast and media partners to develop this with us, starting with a pilot.
