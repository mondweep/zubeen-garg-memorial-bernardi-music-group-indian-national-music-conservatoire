Paying from India?

Three things that help

  1. Switch on international online payments for your card

    Most Indian banks keep this switched off by default. Turn it on in your bank’s app (usually under card settings or card controls), then try again.

  2. Pay by card, no PayPal account needed

    After pressing Checkout, choose to pay by debit or credit card. Visa, Mastercard and most Indian credit cards work; RuPay cards may not.

  3. Check the amount in rupees, then confirm with your OTP

    The price is in pounds. You will see the rupee amount before you pay, and your bank will send a one-time password to approve it.
